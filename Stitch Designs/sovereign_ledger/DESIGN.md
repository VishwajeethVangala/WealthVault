## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the quiet confidence, authority, and discretion of private institutional banking reimagined for modern wealth intelligence. It shuns the hyperactive visual noise of retail trading terminals in favor of contemplative clarity, architectural precision, and serene balance.

### Design Movement
An editorial fusion of **Contemporary Minimalist Utility** and **Private Wealth Prestige**. The interface leverages expansive whitespace, strict tabular rhythm, and quiet tonal contrast. The interplay between an authoritative serif display typeface and a razor-sharp, technocratic grotesque body establishes high-conviction legibility and bespoke sophistication.

### Emotional Demeanor
- **Sovereign Trust:** Stable, anchored, and immutable.
- **Intellectual Restraint:** Functional clarity where data speaks without synthetic drama or gamified stimulation.
- **Architectural Symmetry:** Crisp planar card surfaces resting on subtle muted grounds, bounded by gossamer hairline boundaries.

## Colors

The palette operates under absolute chromatic discipline. Pure black is forbidden; in its place, deep maritime slate anchors critical typography and core actions. Color is strictly functional, never decorative.

### Surface Architecture
- **Base Canvas (`#F8F9FA`):** An airy, muted foundation that prevents glare and eye fatigue during prolonged analytical sessions.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure crystalline white reserved for cards, sheets, and modular panels to provide soft elevation.
- **Hairline Dividers (`#E2E8F0`):** Single-pixel perimeter strokes that articulate structure without visual clutter.

### Semantic Accents
Semantic colors are confined strictly to data metrics, performance telemetry, and transactional state confirmation:
- **Positive Gain (`#059669` / `#10B981`):** Deep emerald for asset appreciation and positive yields.
- **Negative Drawdown (`#DC2626`):** Understated crimson for negative delta, risk triggers, and capital outflow.
- **Interactive State Neutral (`#1E293B`):** Deep charcoal slate for active filters, selected tabs, and primary triggers.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear distinction between narrative authority and quantitative rigor.

### Font Roles & Pairings
- **Display & Section Headers (`Source Serif 4`):** Infuses page headings, portfolio titles, and executive balance overviews with institutional gravitas. Set strictly in normal to medium weights.
- **Telemetry, Controls & Running Text (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Provides clinical, neutral clarity for telemetry, interface mechanics, multi-currency values, and structural labels.

### Numerical Typography Standard
All financial metrics, currency displays, asset ratios, and delta badges must be rendered using tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums`). This guarantees perfect vertical column alignment across balance sheets, allocation tables, and performance matrices.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial engine follows a strict 8-point base grid (with a 4-point micro-scale for compact form inputs and badge padding). Space is treated as an active architectural element to project composure.

### Layout Framework
- **Grid Structure:** Fluid 12-column system capped at a maximum width of `1440px` to maintain optimal data scanning distances on ultra-wide screens.
- **Desktop Margins:** `48px` global lateral padding with `24px` gutter spacing between modules.
- **Tablet & Mobile Adaptation:** Collapses to 6 columns at `<1024px` (with `24px` margins) and to a single/dual column arrangement at `<640px` (with `16px` margins).
- **Density Controls:** High-density table modes reduce vertical cell padding from `space-md` (`16px`) to `space-xs` (`8px`) while keeping font sizes and line heights anchored.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects deep, theatrical shadows and skeuomorphic gradients in favor of **Tonal Stratification and Micro-Contour Lines**.

### Visual Stacking
1. **Canvas (Ground Zero):** `#F8F9FA`. Completely flat, passive foundation.
2. **Structural Panels & Cards (Elevation 1):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an ultra-fine border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`). Subtly grounded by an ambient micro-shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
3. **Interactive Overlays & Flyout Menus (Elevation 2):** `#FFFFFF` backed by `1px solid #CBD5E1` and a soft ambient diffusion: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
4. **Modal Modifiers (Elevation 3):** Backdrop wash using `#0F172A` set to 15% opacity with an immediate `2px` background blur, framing the central modal with a crisp `1px solid #CBD5E1` outline and `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The geometric signature is grounded and deliberate, utilizing a subtle corner radius (`roundedness: 1`). Elements possess tailored corners that avoid clinical sharp edges without succumbing to playful, consumer-grade bubbles.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Standard UI Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** `4px` (`0.25rem`) border-radius.
- **Containers & Data Cards:** `8px` (`0.5rem`) border-radius (`rounded-lg`).
- **Overlays, Floating Drawers & Modals:** `12px` (`0.75rem`) border-radius (`rounded-xl`).
- **Circular Indicators:** Reserved exclusively for user avatars, status pips, and radial progress anchors.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#1E293B` background with crisp white `#FFFFFF` text, `4px` border radius, and no border. Hover shifts smoothly to `#334155`.
- **Secondary / Filter Button:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px solid #E2E8F0`, `#334155` text, and subtle hover transition to `#F1F5F9`.
- **Ghost / Subtle:** Borderless, transparent background; `#475569` text transitioning to `#0F172A` on hover with a `#F1F5F9` background wash.

### Cards & Ledger Surfaces
- **Master Metrics Card:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, `8px` corner radius, `space-lg` (`24px`) internal padding. Display titles use `Source Serif 4`, while numerical balances utilize `Hanken Grotesk` with tabular figures.
- **Metric Delta Chip:** Pill-styled miniature badge with `4px` radius; positive yields feature `#ECFDF5` fill with `#059669` text; negative drawdowns feature `#FEF2F2` fill with `#DC2626` text.

### Form Inputs & Filter Controls
- **Looker-Style Filter Bar:** Compact `36px` control containers, `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #CBD5E1`. On focus: `1px solid #1E293B` with zero outer glowing rings.
- **Text Inputs:** Neutral slate `#0F172A` value text, `#94A3B8` placeholder text, standard horizontal inset of `12px`.

### Tables & Asset Allocation Lists
- **Header Row:** Understated `#F8F9FA` background, uppercase `label-caps` in `#64748B`, height `38px`, bottom edge lined with `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Data Rows:** Background `#FFFFFF`, transition to `#F8FAFC` on hover. Minimalist hairline horizontal divider between rows. Values are strictly right-aligned for numeric and currency comparison; asset names and metadata remain left-aligned.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `16px` bounds with `1px solid #CBD5E1`. Checked state fills with `#1E293B` presenting an ultra-fine white micro-glyph checkmark or dot.
